What a plumbing visit can cover

A plumber’s work starts with understanding the symptom. You might describe a leak, a drain that is not clearing, or a fixture that is not working as expected. Share where it is happening, when you first noticed it, and whether anything has changed. The plumber can then discuss what needs to be checked and whether more details are needed before scheduling.
